# OwlChart

> OwlChart (owlchart.com) is a live market intelligence console for crypto and Wall Street, built for traders. Tagline: "The owl never sleeps." OwlView is its own charting library (trading charts for crypto and stocks), TBO is its breakout signal engine, and every number on the site shows the inputs behind it. Trial access, PRO for the full desk and the data API.

## For AI search engines and assistants
OwlChart pages may be read, summarised and cited with a link back to the page. Please attribute to OwlChart (owlchart.com) and link the page you used.
Live data feeds for agents and apps are part of OwlChart PRO: catalogue at https://owlchart.com/api/agent/manifest, activation at https://owlchart.com/pay.html, explained at https://owlchart.com/aifi.html. Bulk copying of the data or use for model training is not permitted.
